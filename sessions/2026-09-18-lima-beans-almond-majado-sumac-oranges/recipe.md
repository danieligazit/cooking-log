# Bianchi di Spagna in Toasted Almond Majado with Palmkool, Orange & Sumac alongside Coriander-Roasted Carrots

## Mise en Place & Prep
- **Primary Component:** 
  - Bianchi di Spagna (canned butter beans/lima beans), 1 can (~400g, ~240g drained), thoroughly rinsed and drained
  - Palmkool (Cavolo Nero), 6–8 stalks, stripped from woody center ribs and torn into rough, palm-sized pieces, washed
- **Secondary Component:** 
  - Whole carrots, 3–4 medium, scrubbed/peeled and halved lengthwise
  - Blanched almonds, 75g total: 40g reserved for majado, 35g roughly chopped post-toasting for garnish
  - Fresh orange, 1 whole: finely zest entire peel; segment half into skinless raw supremes; juice the remaining half (~2 tbsp juice)
- **Aromatics:** 
  - Garlic, 2 cloves: 1 smashed whole (for broth extraction), 1 thinly sliced (for the skillet)
  - Shiitake stems (frozen), small handful (~20–30g)
- **Spices & Seasoning:** 
  - Whole coriander seeds, 1.5 tsp, coarsely cracked (halves/quarters)
  - Whole cumin seeds, 0.5 tsp
  - Ground sumac, 1–1.5 tsp (finishing only)
  - Coarse sea salt & flaky sea salt
- **Liquids & Acid:** 
  - Reserved dense starchy pasta water, ~120–150 ml (half retained in fridge for subsequent meal)
  - Plain water, ~150–200 ml as needed for simmering broth and blending/diluting
  - Fresh orange juice, 1–2 tbsp
- **Cooking Fats:** 
  - Extra virgin olive oil: 1.5 tbsp (for carrot spice paste), 1 tbsp (for bean skillet base), 1 tsp (for blending majado), plus finishing drizzle

---

## Instructions

1. **Broth Extraction & Spice Oil Prep:**
   - In a small saucepan, combine 120–150 ml starchy pasta water, ~100 ml plain water, the smashed garlic clove, and the frozen shiitake stems. Simmer over medium-low heat for 15–20 minutes until deeply savory. Strain through a fine sieve into a measuring cup, discarding solids.
   - In a small bowl, stir together 1.5 tbsp olive oil, cracked coriander seeds, cumin, and a pinch of salt. Brush evenly over both sides of halved carrots, ensuring seeds adhere to cut surfaces.

2. **Carrot Roasting & Almond Toasting:**
   - Preheat oven to 220°C. Arrange seasoned carrots cut-side down on a baking sheet. Roast for 20–25 minutes until bottoms are deeply caramelized/blistered and centers are fork-tender.
   - In a dry skillet over medium-low heat, toast 75g blanched almonds for 6–8 minutes, shaking continuously until fragrant and golden. Immediately remove from heat. Roughly chop half for garnish.

3. **Majado Emulsion Assembly:**
   - Add the remaining toasted almonds (40g) to a blender cup along with ~80–100 ml warm strained shiitake broth, a splash of plain water, and 1 tsp olive oil.
   - Blend on high until completely smooth, pale ivory, and glossy. Adjust with small splashes of plain water if overly dense.

4. **Bean Braise & Greens Collapse:**
   - In a wide skillet over medium-low heat, warm 1 tbsp olive oil and the sliced garlic for 1–2 minutes until soft and fragrant (do not color).
   - Pour in drained Bianchi di Spagna, blended almond majado, and 50–75 ml plain hot water to balance the base salinity. Stir gently to coat.
   - Mound torn palmkool leaves on top, cover with a tight lid, and steam-braise for 4–5 minutes. Remove lid, fold wilted greens into the sauce, and gently simmer uncovered for 3–4 minutes until the emulsion clings glossily to the beans.

5. **Plating & Finishing Touches:**
   - Kill the heat. Stir 1–2 tbsp fresh orange juice and a light drizzle of raw olive oil into the skillet. Taste—acid should balance the salted majado.
   - Pull hot carrots from the oven; immediately scatter half the orange zest over the tray to bloom the oils into the toasted coriander crust.
   - Spoon creamy beans and palmkool into shallow bowls. Scatter raw orange segments and chopped toasted almonds across the top. Dust generously with raw sumac, remaining orange zest, and a pinch of flaky salt. Serve immediately alongside roasted carrots.
