# Session Retrospective: 2026-09-18

## Verdict
* **Rating:** 5.0 / 5.0
* High-level triumph across both plates: luxurious, creamy mouthfeel anchored by bitter brassica minerality, balanced cleanly by sweet-tart citrus bursts and warm toasted coriander crunch.
* Key balance takeaway: Salinity from the reduced pasta water in the majado was completely reined in and counterpointed by the cold raw orange segments, late orange juice, and bright dusting of raw sumac.
* Diner reception was exceptionally high (Lia loved it); execution flow felt far easier and more approachable than initially anticipated.

---

## Reality & Pan Observations
* **Deviations from Blueprint:** 
  * Diluted the shiitake extraction and the blender cup significantly with plain tap water rather than relying purely on pasta water, preventing catastrophic over-salting and paste-like gummy thickening.
  * Added a small splash of olive oil and extra water into the blender carafe to provide sufficient shearing fluidity for the toasted almonds.
  * Deployed the fresh orange as raw cold segments directly on the plated bowls rather than solely relying on juice/zest, providing essential textural brightness.
* **Texture Analysis:** 
  * Bianchi di Spagna were an absolute win—significantly creamier, softer, and more delicate than standard *witte bonen*, holding their structural skins without chalkiness.
  * Palmkool ended up slightly too chewy and fibrous
* **Heat & Timing Checks:** 
  * Carrots caramelized exceptionally well cut-side down at 220°C with no scorching of the cracked coriander seeds.
  * Majado bound smoothly in the skillet without breaking or oil separation once kept at a gentle simmer.

---

## Sensory Debrief & Adjustments for Next Time
* **Ingredient Adjustments:** 
  * Stick permanently with Bianchi di Spagna over smaller white beans for this preparation.
  * Retain the raw orange supremes as a mandatory plating component—the juicy pop completely transforms the rich almond base.
* **Technique Refinements:** 
  * **Palmkool Breakdown:** Extend the greens braising window to 7–9 minutes. Wilt the palmkool into the pan liquid earlier (simultaneously with the beans, or even 2 minutes prior) rather than steaming briefly at the end.
  * **Leaf Cut:** Slice or tear across the leaf grain slightly smaller to sever long structural veins prior to hitting the pan.
  * **Majado Moisture:** Start the blender carafe with a 2:1 ratio of water/broth to nuts to avoid motor stalling and rapid paste formation before emulsification occurs.
* **Structural Pairing:** 
  * The coriander-cumin roasted carrots provided an ideal earthy, sweet counterweight. For a starch variation, thick slices of crusty pan-fried sourdough rubbed with raw garlic would be equally effective at mopping the sauce.
