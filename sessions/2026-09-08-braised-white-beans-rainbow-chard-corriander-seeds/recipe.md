# Braised Giant White Beans with Rainbow Chard, Coriander Seeds & Sumac

## Mise en Place & Prep
- **White Beans:** 1 large jar or 2 cans (approx. 400–500g drained) giant white beans (butter beans / *witte reuzenbonen*), rinsed and drained.
- **Rainbow Chard:** 1 bunch (approx. 200–250g). Wash thoroughly. Strip leaves from stems. Slice stems crosswise into 5 mm crescents; roughly chop leaves into wide ribbons.
- **Aromatics:** 1 medium yellow onion or 1 large leek (thinly sliced), 3 cloves garlic (thinly sliced).
- **Spices:** 1 tsp coriander seeds (crushed), 1 tsp sumac (plus extra for finishing), pinch of chili flakes (optional).
- **Liquid & Acid:** 120ml (½ cup) light vegetable stock or water, 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice.
- **Fat:** 2–3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil.
- **Seasoning:** Fine sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper.

---

## Instructions

1. **Pre-Cook Chard Stems:**
   - Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide skillet over medium-low heat.
   - Add the sliced chard stems with a splash of water and a pinch of salt.
   - Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes until completely tender and yielding.

2. **Sweat Alliums:**
   - Add another 1 tbsp olive oil and the sliced onion or leek.
   - Sauté alongside the softened stems for 6–8 minutes until soft and translucent.

3. **Bloom Aromatics:**
   - Clear a well in the center of the pan.
   - Add a splash of olive oil, the sliced garlic, crushed coriander seeds, and chili flakes.
   - Sizzle in the warm oil pool for 45–60 seconds until fragrant and pale gold, then fold through the vegetables.

4. **Incorporate Beans & Simmer:**
   - Add the drained giant white beans and 120ml stock or water.
   - Simmer gently for 6–8 minutes.
   - Lightly crush a small spoonful of beans against the pan bottom to emulsify the liquid into a creamy sauce.

5. **Wilt Chard Leaves:**
   - Fold in the chopped chard leaves in batches.
   - Cook for 2–3 minutes until just wilted and vibrant green.

6. **Finish & Brighten:**
   - Take the pan off the heat.
   - Stir in 1 tbsp sumac and the lemon juice.
   - Adjust salt and pepper, drizzle with raw olive oil, and dust generously with sumac before serving.
