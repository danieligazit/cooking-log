## Reality & Pivots in the Pan
* **Fennel Prep:** Wouldn't crush under a mug or glass on the board; added whole directly to the center oil pool instead. Bloomed beautifully and softened gently in the fat.
* **Fig Ratio:** Scaled up to 6 figs (recipe called for 4). Held up nicely without overwhelming the savory elements.
* **Heat Control on Leek 1:** Burned the braised leek cylinders slightly during the initial sear, but it was barely noticeable once braised down.
* **Oil Usage:** Ended up needing more oil than the recipe called for to get the vegetables cooking and sweating the way that felt right.

---

## Sensory Debrief & Adjustments for Next Time
* **Oil Balance & Sides:** The finished plate felt a bit too oily by the end due to the extra additions. Either balance with some water when heat controlling leaks or alternatively simply pair this with an oil-absorbing side dish (e.g., plain boiled or lightly crushed baby potatoes) to capture the seasoned pan fats.
* **Shiitake Cut:** Cut cap strips slightly thicker/wider next time so they keep even more body and chew after the water cooks out.
* **Portioning:** Total yield came out smaller than anticipated—scale the base aromatics/mushrooms if serving as a heavy main rather than an appetizer/small plate.
