# Braised Leek Confit with Shiitake, Fig & Leek Sofrito

## Mise en Place & Prep
- **Leek 1 (Braised Base):** 1 whole stalk. Trim roots and dark tough green tops. Discard outermost layer, rinse exterior clean, and cut pale stalk into three 6–7 cm cylinders.
- **Leek 2 (Sofrito):** 1 whole stalk. Trim roots and tops. Halve lengthwise, fan layers under cold tap water to flush grit, and slice crosswise into 2–3 mm half-moons.
- **Shiitake Mushrooms:** 200g (2 packs). Clean with dry towel, discard woody stems, and slice caps into strips.
- **Fresh Figs:** 4–6 ripe figs, quartered lengthwise.
- **Aromatics & Acid:** 2 cloves garlic (thinly sliced), 1 level tsp whole fennel seeds, 1 tbsp vinegar (sherry, balsamic, or apple cider).
- **Fat & Liquid:** Extra virgin olive oil (~4 tbsp total), 60ml water.

---

## Instructions

1. **Char & Steam-Braise Leek 1 (Cylinders):**
   - Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high.
   - Sear leek cylinders undisturbed for 3–4 minutes per side until deeply caramelized.
   - Drop heat to low, add 60ml water, cover tightly with a lid, and braise for 15–18 minutes until custardy. Transfer to plates; wipe pan.

2. **Dry-Sear Shiitakes:**
   - Return pan to medium-high with no oil.
   - Add sliced caps with a pinch of salt. Cook dry for 2 minutes until moisture evaporates.
   - Add 1 tbsp olive oil; sauté 2 minutes until edges are crisp and deep golden-brown. Set aside.

3. **Melt Leek 2 (Sofrito):**
   - Reduce heat to medium-low, add 1 tbsp olive oil, sliced leeks, and a pinch of salt.
   - Sweat slowly for ~8 minutes until translucent, collapsed, and jammy.

4. **Bloom Aromatics:**
   - Clear a well in the center of the leeks.
   - Add sliced garlic and whole fennel seeds directly into the oil pool. Sizzle for 45–60 seconds until fragrant and pale gold, then fold through the leeks.

5. **Blister Figs & Glaze:**
   - Push sofrito to pan edges; turn heat to medium.
   - Place figs cut-side down in the center for 60–90 seconds to caramelize.
   - Return shiitakes to pan, drizzle with 1 tbsp vinegar, and gently swirl/toss for 30 seconds as the liquid bubbles and glazes. Remove from heat immediately.

6. **Assemble:**
   - Plate braised cylinders, spoon hot sofrito, shiitakes, and figs over and around. Finish with flaky sea salt and coarse black pepper.
